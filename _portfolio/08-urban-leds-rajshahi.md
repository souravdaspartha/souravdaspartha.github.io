**Image Research and Consultancy Ltd. | Funded by UN-Habitat | 2018 – 2021**
**Role:** Junior Urban Planner

## What I did

- Tree mapping by GPS data collection and identifying trees by following the tree catalog.
- Preparing handbook consisting of detailed information about trees.
- Natural asset mapping by creating shapefiles after rectifying google images and field verification.
- Ecosystem service mapping to find out the people who are getting services from different types of ecosystems by collecting data through transect walk and community consultation.
- Open green space planning for making the assets more accessible and making future plan on how to increase the service area.

**Tools:** ArcGIS, QGIS, GPS survey
