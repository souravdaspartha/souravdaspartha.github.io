**Engineering and Planning Consultants Ltd. | DSCC, funded by World Bank | Sep 2021 – Aug 2022**
**Role:** Junior Transport Planner

## What I did

- Reviewed key documents and secondary data, including RSTP, DUTP, DHUTS, DAP, and relevant codes.
- Managed various traffic surveys, ensuring accurate data collection and analysis for transport planning.
- Conducted baseline data analysis covering traffic, pedestrian, and public transport data.
- Identified study routes, intersections, and land use using ArcGIS, and prepared bottleneck maps.
- Assisted in Traffic Management Plan (TMP) preparation and prioritized bottleneck points.
- Performed network analysis and calculated Level of Service (LOS) and Average Daily Traffic (ADT).
- Developed Origin-Destination (OD) matrices, designed intersections in Civil 3D, and prepared maps of existing roads.

**Tools:** Civil 3D, ArcGIS, AutoCAD
