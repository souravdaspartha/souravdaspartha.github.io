**Image Research and Consultancy Ltd. | Funded by Solidarités International | Oct 2018 – Jun 2020**
**Role:** Junior Urban Planner

## Activities Performed

- Boundary demarcation by using hand GPS and google earth.
- GCP point collection for google earth image rectification.
- Utility location collection in point and polygon shapefile by integrating digitization from google earth image and field verification.
- Preparing base map after collecting all the utility location data and preparing final database based on this.
- Analyzing served area, unserved area, drain inundation area etc.

**Tools:** ArcGIS, QGIS, GPS survey
